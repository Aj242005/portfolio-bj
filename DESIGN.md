# Design System: "The Signal" (Vintage Radio Edition)

## 1. Visual World & Direction

- **Concept**: "The Signal" — An interactive, diegetic 1940s tabletop tube radio receiver. Represents post-quantum cryptographic signals piercing through analog atmospheric noise.
- **Atmosphere**: Rich dark walnut wood, warm incandescent lamp glow, polished brass hardware, glowing tungsten filaments, emerald phosphor green Magic Eye tube, and distant broadcast transmission towers against a deep night sky (`#0e0a07`).
- **Philosophy**: Warm, tactile, nostalgic, and visually captivating, while remaining crystal-clear, legible, and frictionless for recruiters, engineers, and hiring managers. Never gates the resume behind the 3D interaction.

---

## 3. Typography Hierarchy

- **Headings & Display**: `Special Elite` (vintage mechanical typewriter font), warm cream tones (`#e8dcc8`), tracking and character spacing reminiscent of 1940s radio manuals.
- **Body & Data Labels**: `IBM Plex Mono` (monospace precision), crisp contrast (`#e8dcc8` on `#1a1410`), clear telemetry labels.
- **Telemetry Readouts**: Tabular numerals for frequencies (MHz), timestamps, throughput (TPS), and latency metrics.

---

## 4. Components & Mechanics

### 3D Tabletop Radio Receiver
- Handcrafted procedural Art Deco waterfall walnut cabinet with satin brass trim.
- Large illuminated frequency dial window (88.0 to 108.0 MHz) with red sweeping pointer needle.
- Functional Magic Eye 6E5 cathode ray tube that reacts dynamically to signal proximity.
- 3 exposed glass vacuum tubes on the top chassis with glowing orange tungsten filament coils.
- Large knurled brass tuning knob with click & drag + mouse wheel scrolling.
- Clickable volume knob that toggles audio mute with an active red power pilot lamp.

### Broadcast Transmission Towers
- 8 vintage lattice transmission towers arranged in an arc across the horizon.
- Heavy concrete footings, lattice pylons with safety tiers and aviation hazard lights.
- Summit beacon orbs with un-tone-mapped bloom halo flares.
- 3 concentric animated radio wave rings expanding outward into the sky.
- Vertical ionospheric light pillar when tuned / locked.
- Clickable in 3D to instantly tune to that station.

### Station Cards
- 2D overlay positioned on the right side of the screen with warm parchment / vintage radio panel styling.
- Clear hierarchy: frequency callsign -> role / dates -> project summary -> key performance metrics -> technology tags -> resume CTA.

### Persistent Plain Resume
- 1-click CTA always visible in the header: "VIEW RESUME" (`/resume.html`).
- Semantic, printer-friendly HTML, zero JavaScript dependency, WCAG AA contrast.
